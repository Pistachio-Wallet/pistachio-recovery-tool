Pistachio recovery tool

Reach your funds with only your backup — no Pistachio account, app, or servers required.

Never share your key or phrase. Anyone who has it controls your funds. No support agent, website, or person ever needs it.
Your key stays in this page. It is used only to sign, right here in your browser, and is never uploaded. The tool goes online only to read your balances and broadcast your sweep — never to send your key. It is open source, so you can confirm this yourself.
Download it, then open your own copy. Get this file from the official release, check its SHA-256 checksum, and open the saved file from your computer. It opens in your browser like any page — that is expected; the point is that it is your verified copy running locally, not a live website a link could tamper with.

On any row that shows a balance, press Sweep, paste a destination address you control, and confirm. The address is shown back to you before anything is sent.

How gas is paid. Plain wallets pay the fee out of the funds being swept — nothing extra to do. A smart account pays its own fee from its balance, but its owner wallet submits the transaction, so first send a little native gas to the owner address on that chain (it is reimbursed from the account).
