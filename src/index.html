Never share your key or phrase. Anyone who has it controls your funds. No support agent, website, or person ever needs it.
Your key stays in this page. It is used only to sign, right here in your browser, and is never uploaded. The tool connects to the internet only to read your balances and broadcast your sweep — never to send your key. It is open source, so you can confirm that yourself.
Download it, don't trust a link. Get this file from the official release and check its SHA-256 checksum, then open the saved file locally. A hosted page can be tampered with; a verified file cannot. Deriving your address works fully offline; reading balances and sweeping need a connection.
